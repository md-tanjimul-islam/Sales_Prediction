    How it works

    1
    Clean the dataRemove ID columns, duplicates, missing and invalid values; check outliers and skew.
    2
    Engineer featuresTV×Radio interaction, squared terms and log(TV) capture synergy and diminishing returns.
    3
    Compare modelsLinear, Ridge, Lasso, ElasticNet, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting, tuned and cross-validated.
    4
    Explain predictionsEach forecast comes with a range, channel contributions and what-if budget moves.

    The data is observational, so impact figures are estimates rather than guaranteed causal effects. Predictions outside the training ranges are less reliable.
